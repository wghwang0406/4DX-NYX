View the signed-in account's balance, subscription plan, and recent credit transactions.

Usage:
  higgsfield account [command]

Aliases:
  account, acc

Examples:
  higgsfield account status
  higgsfield account transactions --size 50

Available Commands:
  status       Show account email, plan, and available credits
  transactions List recent credit transactions

Flags:
  -h, --help   help for account

Global Flags:
      --json       print raw JSON responses
      --no-color   disable color output

Use "higgsfield account [command] --help" for more information about a command.
